TERMS OF SERVICE

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

1. Membership Agreement & Acceptance

By registering as a member, purchasing a workout package, or utilizing facility equipment at MULTIGYM Mohammadpur (24/3 Taj Mahal Road, Shia Masjid Mor, Mohammadpur, Dhaka 1207), you agree to comply fully with these Terms of Service.

Management reserves the right to modify admission fees, operating schedules, or floor safety rules to maintain high quality and security for all members.

2. Membership Plans & Payment Terms

  • Package Tiers: Standard monthly membership is 1,500/-, 3 Months Package is 5,000/-, 6 Months Package is 8,500/-, and 12 Months Package is 15,000/-.
  • Non-Transferable & Non-Refundable: Admission fees and package subscriptions are non-transferable and non-refundable once activated, unless specified by official written management consent.
  • Front Desk Verification: All online package rates, promotional posts, or website notices must be verified at the front desk before final registration payment.

3. Gents & Ladies Floor Etiquette

MULTIGYM operates dedicated, separate floors for Gents and Ladies to ensure privacy and comfort:

  • Proper Fitness Attire: Members must wear proper sports shoes, breathable workout wear, and carry a clean workout towel at all times. Outdoor street shoes are strictly prohibited on the workout floor.
  • Weight Racking & Care: Plates, dumbbells, and cable attachments must be re-racked after completing each set. Dropping heavy weights excessively is prohibited.
  • Ladies Floor Privacy: Certified female trainers handle female instruction on dedicated floors. Privacy of female members is strictly preserved.

4. Lockers & Steam Bath Safety

Members are entitled to use individual lockers during their workout session. Lockers must be emptied before leaving the premises daily.

Access to complimentary steam bath facilities is subject to posted health guidelines and scheduled operating hours. Members with high blood pressure or cardiac conditions must consult their physician before using steam rooms.

5. Disclaimer of Accuracy & Health Liability

Important Notice: All information, offers, prices, and images shown on this website or post may not be 100% accurate and may include dummy content. Please verify all details with the gym front desk before joining.

Exercise and physical training carry inherent physical risks. Members participate in physical workouts at their own discretion. MULTIGYM master trainers provide exercise chart guidance, but members are responsible for disclosing pre-existing medical conditions.

6. Code of Conduct & Suspension

MULTIGYM maintains zero tolerance for harassment, verbal abuse, damage to equipment, or misconduct toward instructors, staff, or fellow members.

Management reserves the right to suspend or revoke membership without refund in the event of repeated violation of gym rules.
